Review revision 5 against THAT design: verify the table is closed (no `.*`/`.+`/free tails), that all rev1–rev4 probes refuse after one fetch at the production entry, that the "environmental noise" positive test only uses table lines, and that the two named mutants are killed. The Windows-refusal choice for P2 (typed refusal before process creation, Windows-only test file) was accepted in rev4 and is not re-opened. Do not require support for diagnostics outside the table; a missing real-world shape is a follow-up note, not a finding, unless it makes the resolver refuse a valid availability failure the acceptance names (DNS, connection refused/timed out).
